Recipe

The exact process, in plain language

Forming the seed:
1. Take the poll number (public, decided before the call) and the exact click timestamp (captured live, posted in chat).
2. Combine them as text: seed_input = pollNumber + ":" + timestamp.
3. Run that through SHA-256 to get a scrambled 64-character hash. Take its first 8 characters as a number — this is the numeric seed that drives everything below.

Preparing the lists:
4. Sort the team list alphabetically. Sort the player list alphabetically by name. Group players into four categories in this fixed order: Goalkeepers → Defenders (CB, LB, RB, LWB, RWB, SW) → Midfielders (CDM/DM, CM, CAM/AM, LM, RM) → Forwards (LW, RW, SS, CF, ST), and within each category, into the exact sub-positions in that order.

Distributing players:
5. Track two running counts per team: their count within the CURRENT category (resets to zero at the start of every category), and their OVERALL total across the whole draft so far (never resets).
6. Go through the categories in order (GK, then Defenders, then Midfielders, then Forwards), and within each category, go through its sub-positions in order (e.g. within Defenders: CB, then LB, then RB...).
7. For each sub-position: shuffle its players using the numeric seed. Then hand them out in rounds — each round, generate a fresh random order of teams (using the same ongoing seed), then sort that order by: first, whoever has the fewest players in the CURRENT category; and among any teams tied on that, whoever has the fewest players OVERALL. Give one player per team, in that order, until either everyone has one or the sub-position's players run out.
8. This means: as long as there are enough players in a sub-position for every team, everyone gets one before anyone gets a second — no team is ever 2 ahead of another within a single category (Goalkeepers, Defenders, Midfielders, or Forwards). Whenever there's a genuine choice to make (a remainder, or a scarce sub-position), it goes to whichever eligible team is furthest behind on total squad size — this is what keeps total squad sizes converging to equal without breaking the category rule.

Result:
9. Repeat step 7–8 for every sub-position, in every category, until all players are placed. The final rosters are exactly reproducible by anyone who has the same poll number, timestamp, player list, and team list — that's what the Verifier tab does.
Manual check

Reconfirming the seed without this page at all

Since SHA-256 is a public, standard algorithm, the seed itself can be recomputed on any device, with no dependency on this tool.
Python:
python3 -c "import hashlib; print(hashlib.sha256('POLLNUMBER:TIMESTAMP'.encode()).hexdigest())"
Mac/Linux terminal:
echo -n "POLLNUMBER:TIMESTAMP" | shasum -a 256
Compare the first 8 characters of the output — that's the numeric seed (read as hexadecimal) used to drive the entire draft.
